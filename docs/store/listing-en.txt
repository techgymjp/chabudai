"I can't eat this!"

Grab your phone, swing it up hard, and watch a grumpy bald dad flip his chabudai — a low Japanese dining table — clear across a shot put stadium. How far can you send it? Go for the world record!

■ USE YOUR WHOLE BODY
• Swing power (accelerometer) and wrist snap (gyroscope) set the launch speed
• Spin around once like a shot putter for extra distance
• Shout! Your voice volume becomes "spirit" (the mic only measures loudness)
• Your swing angle decides the launch angle
• Throw toward this year's lucky direction (Ehō) for a bonus

■ WATCH IT FLY
• Rice, miso soup, grilled fish and teacups fly everywhere and smash on landing
• The camera follows the table high into the sky; tailwinds and headwinds matter
• Stray too far off center and it's a foul
• Once in a while, it's Dad who goes flying instead of the table…

■ FEATURES
• Ranks from "Barely touched the table" to "Legendary Table Flip God"
• Tips on the result screen show which part of your throw to improve
• Share your best distance and rank with friends
• Touch controls for devices without motion sensors
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese

Hold your phone tight so it doesn't fly out of your hand, and make sure there's nothing and no one around you.
